Which Devices Support AR?

Smart Glasses & Hands/Free AR

They're one of the most expensive way to get into AR, but at the same time they provide one of the most inmersive experiences.

Interactive Smart Mirrors

They're one of the most innovative ways of testing out clothes nowadays.

Smartphones & Mobile AR

The most affordable way to get into AR but the least immersive. It can be used to see how products will look in your room but it has many limitations.
