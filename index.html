AR Assistant

Augmented Reality is a tool that lets us see 3D elements through a real-time camera, it can also help us interact.


Our challenge

Problem: Students interacting with electronics and delicate equipment don't always know or are aware about the safety measures when working with them, which may result in damages to expensive devices.

Solution: Because of this we can use AR in our favor, by using AR glasses students will be able to interact with electronics from subjects like IT-Hardware. They will be having a tutorial on safety measures and be able to experiment as much as they want, since any damage won't be reflected in expensive repairs or replacements.


Limitations

Current Limitations:

  • Development Scope: Due to time constraints, as well as lack of knowledge of Javascript we cannot implement a bunch of functinality into the website, such as making it more interactive and user-friendly.
  • Live Prototyping: Developing an actual AR software would be neat and useful to learn in more detail how it works and how it can be used to benefit us as students, but we would be needing to learn more about.
  • Hardware Constraints: Our current devices are fairly simple and cannot handle complicated and demanding stuff like developing an actual sophisticated AR that will satisfy the necessities of our target audience.
