How we use AR!

How is Augmented Reality Different?

We use AR to bridge the gap between amateurs and professionals. Because we all are beginners when it comes to new hardware, and because new Hardware is sensitive and Expensive we can use AR to point us in the right direction

Key Features of Our Idea

  • Step-by-step Visual Instructions
  • Component Identification
  • Real-time safety warnings
  • Live Equipment Data
  • Remote Expert Assistance

User Journey

  1. Users check the compatibility of their device with the AR glasses
  2. They insert the smartphone in the AR glasses and perform any system installations if needed
  3. They point at the equipment that they intend to use and the AR glasses using an integrated software detect the equipment and direct the users as to what needs to be done next. The specific instructions depend on how the AR glasses are programmed
  4. While taking the assistance of the AR, the user performs the step-by-step intrusctions even when they are not an expert themselves

    5. Augmented Reality is a tool that lets us see 3D elements through a real-time camera, it can also help us interact.
